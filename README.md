# Moye Notes

Handwrite and highlight on PDFs, then export annotated copies. Create handwritten notebooks in the same Obsidian vault. Moye Notes Free works offline and is free to use without an account or activation.

### Discover Moye Notes Pro

Moye Free offers essential handwriting and PDF annotation. **Moye Pro goes further, connecting handwritten notes, PDF and Markdown annotations, and knowledge management inside Obsidian.**

- **Markdown Handwritten Annotation:** Write and highlight directly on Markdown notes, collect excerpts, jump back to source passages, and export annotated notes as PDF.
- **Advanced PDF Annotation:** Collect and categorize annotations, navigate back to original passages, extend writing space, and use professional annotation tools.
- **Professional Handwriting:** Pressure-sensitive fountain and ballpoint pens with refined writing controls.
- **Handwriting OCR & Beautification:** Recognize handwriting, convert it into editable text, and improve handwritten note presentation.
- **Creative Notebooks:** Sticky notes, decorative tapes, stickers, shapes, text boxes, image editing, rich paper templates, and custom covers.
- **AI Q&A:** Ask questions about selected text or regions of your notes for explanations, translations, and summaries.
- **Obsidian Integration:** Combine handwritten notebooks with embedded Markdown content and connected excerpts.

Moye Pro is a separate paid edition. Free remains fully usable without purchasing Pro.

## PDF annotation

- Write and highlight with the ballpoint pen and highlighter. Use the eraser, ruler, or image insertion as needed.
- Find and select text, and navigate with the outline and page thumbnails. Moye remembers the last page opened on this device.
- Show or hide your annotations, or export them in a new PDF. Your annotations are saved alongside the original PDF, which stays intact.

## Handwritten notebooks

- Choose blank, grid, or dotted paper. Browse a single page, a spread, or a vertical stack.
- Use the basic ballpoint pen, hold to shape, and switch between freehand and straight-line highlighting.
- Find and favorite notebooks in the Moye library. Export notebooks as PDF and recover copies from local backups.

## Get started

1. In Obsidian, open **Settings → Community plugins → Browse**. Search for **Moye Notes**, then install and enable it.
2. Open a PDF in your vault. Choose the Moye annotation icon in its header or run **Open current PDF in Moye** from the command palette.
3. Run **Open the Moye library**, then select **New** to create a notebook. Moye settings let you choose save locations and switch to Simplified Chinese.

Requires Obsidian 1.15.1 or later. PDF rendering on iPhone and iPad requires iOS or iPadOS 18 or later. JPEG 2000 decoding is included and works offline.

## Files and privacy

Notebooks, PDF annotations, and their attached images are stored in your vault. Keep the original PDF, its companion annotation file, and any attached images together when backing up or moving a PDF. Automatic recovery backups stay in Obsidian's local storage on the current device.

Moye Notes Free has no telemetry or account requirement. Writing, PDF rendering, search, and export work offline. You can use the system file picker to insert an image from outside your vault. Support links open your browser or email app when you select them. In **Upgrade to Pro**, choose **Pay with PayPal** to open PayPal’s checkout in your browser.
